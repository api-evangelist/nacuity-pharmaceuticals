# Nacuity Pharmaceuticals

> Nacuity Pharmaceuticals, Inc. is a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company in Fort Worth, Texas,
> with operations in Carlton South, Victoria, Australia. It develops therapies that target oxidative
> stress, the mechanism it holds responsible for a family of blinding eye diseases. Its lead asset is
> NPI-001 (N-acetylcysteine amide) tablets for retinitis pigmentosa, which holds FDA Breakthrough
> Therapy, Fast Track and Orphan Drug designations; a second programme, NPI-002, is an intravitreal
> implant for the delay of cataract progression. A third programme addresses cystinosis. The company
> is backed by Foundation Fighting Blindness and its venture arm the RD Fund, and licensed its
> founding technology from Johns Hopkins University.

users, settings, menus, themes, plugins, templates, the wp-abilities/v1 capability registry,
wordfence/v1, wp-site-health/v1 and the administrative half of yoast/v1 and exactmetrics/v1 —

- [Media library](https://www.nacuity.com/wp-json/wp/v2/media): 140 attachments — logos, headshots, scientific figures.

- [oEmbed provider](https://www.nacuity.com/wp-json/oembed/1.0/embed): oEmbed 1.0 rich responses for nacuity.com URLs.
- [Sitemap index](https://www.nacuity.com/sitemap_index.xml): Yoast sitemap index.

### Read this before consuming the content API

`content.rendered` and `excerpt.rendered` are EMPTY STRINGS on all 29 published pages. The page
bodies are authored in Slider Revolution / page-builder post meta that WordPress does not project
into the REST content field. **The API will not give you the text of a Nacuity press release.** To
read a release, fetch its HTML `link`. What the API does reliably give you is identity and hierarchy
(id, slug, title, link, date, modified, parent, menu_order), the media library, and the per-page
`yoast_head_json` object carrying the canonical URL, robots directives, Open Graph tags and the
schema.org `@graph` (WebPage, BreadcrumbList, WebSite, Organization).

Pagination is `page` + `per_page` (1-100) with totals in the `X-WP-Total` and `X-WP-TotalPages`
headers — the body is a bare array, so a client reading only the body cannot tell if more pages
exist. Use `_fields` to trim responses; the unfiltered page object carries a large `yoast_head`
markup string. Errors are the WordPress `{code, message, data}` envelope served as
`application/json` — NOT RFC 9457 problem+json. No rate-limit headers are advertised; be
conservative.
